# Burger Life

Cat food with burger-counter flavor language. The buyer is a person who feeds a cat and is waiting for the first sale, not a diner customer.

## Color

Tokens are named for the counter, then mapped onto shadcn variables in `src/index.css`.

- Tray `#F5C518` — saturated mustard, first screen only. Heat-lamp field, not a cream page.
- Patty `#3B2418` — ink and primary buttons. Dark brown, not near-black chrome.
- Ketchup `#C81D15` — one accent for the “not on sale yet” mark. Not a clay/terracotta CTA.
- Pickle `#1F6B45` — the feline constraint (balanced for a cat). Not acid green on black.
- Wrapper `#FFFDF8` — bag paper for the rest of the page.
- Grill `#1A120E` — footer and the darkest type on the tray.

Rejected defaults: warm cream plus terracotta, near-black plus acid green, hairline broadsheet, identical shadowed cards.

## Type

- Display: Unbounded, weight 700. A wide Cyrillic poster face for the name and section titles.
- Body: Golos Text, weights 400 and 500. Label text for a Russian reader, line length under 80 characters, line-height about 1.6.

Bevan and Atkinson Hyperlegible were the first pair and were dropped because neither file covers Cyrillic. Unbounded keeps the menu-board weight. Golos Text keeps the feeding-label job.

## Layout

Left aligned. The first screen is a tray: claim on the left, one paper slip on the right. Below the tray the page is a bag label: flavor rows in one list, composition as two columns, subscription as a real sequence, FAQ, then the launch form. One radius for controls; sections are not a grid of equal cards.

## Principles

The memorable thing is the tray and a single spotlight behind the name. Everything after it stays still. Copy states only what the brief supports. Missing studies, reviews, certificates, recipes, and commercial terms stay marked as content requirements.
